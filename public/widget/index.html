Oyi AI Communication Center

The living interface for Ochiga conversations.

Oyi is the parent AI layer. It listens, speaks, routes to Oma, Osa, support, sales, and future specialist agents, then docks into the chat widget when a dashboard-style conversation is needed.

Tap the orb to wake Oyi and open the communication widget.
What it is The Oyi AI doorway for websites, estate pages, support flows, and sales surfaces.
What it does Talks, listens, receives plans/files, qualifies needs, and routes the conversation to Oma, Osa, or the right Ochiga workflow.
How to embed Add the script below to any website and point it at your backend.
<script src="/widget.js" data-oma-widget="true" data-api-base="https://your-backend.example.com" data-agent-name="Oyi" data-brand-name="Ochiga" data-title="Oyi AI" data-subtitle="Communication center for estates, smart buildings, support, and agents"></script>
