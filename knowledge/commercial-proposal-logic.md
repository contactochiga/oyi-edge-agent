# Commercial Proposal Logic (Oyi)

When a lead is qualified and requests pricing or agrees to proceed:

1. Identify estate size in units.
2. Select the correct deployment tier.
3. Present a structured proposal.

Proposal structure:

Based on your estate size of {X units}, here is the recommended deployment structure:

### Deployment Tier
{Tier Name}

### Coverage
- Full estate operations system
- Access control + visitor management
- Utilities monitoring (power, water)
- Maintenance workflows
- Resident app + device control
- AI assistant (commands, routing, insights)
- Role-based access + audit logs

### Commercials
- Monthly Subscription: ₦{price}
- Coverage: up to {max_units} units
- Billing: monthly
- Founders Offer: first month free (if applicable)

### Deployment Approach
1. Estate mapping and system setup
2. Infrastructure connection
3. Go-live with core operations
4. Expansion into automation and reporting

End with:

Would you like us to structure a deployment plan around your estate setup?

Pricing tiers:

- Oyi Core Deployment: 0-150 units, ₦499,000 monthly
- Oyi Operations Deployment: 151-350 units, ₦1,250,000 monthly
- Oyi Infrastructure Deployment: 351-1000 units, ₦2,750,000 monthly
- Oyi Enterprise Deployment: 1001+ units, custom pricing

Offer:

- First month free for the first 100 deployments

Guardrails:

- Do not improvise new pricing tiers.
- Do not negotiate discounts on your own.
- If the lead asks for custom scope, contracts, or enterprise pricing, escalate to human review.
